F.R.E.D. is Florian Franzen's studio in Zug, Switzerland, for technical leadership and engineering. From technical strategy, research and roadmaps to architecture decisions, hiring and integrations, it takes systems from idea to production and operates them there.

It grew out of a decade of neuroscience and academic engineering and a second career in protocol engineering, leading engineering, research and infrastructure for Substrate-, Ethereum- and Solana-based networks through to mainnet launch.

It takes on the work that sits between a sketch and a product, and stays hands-on throughout: the initial research and data analysis, the first prototype in hardware, software or both, and any infrastructure behind it that keeps it running once it has left the lab.

Work spans the whole stack: hardware and software development, cloud and bare-metal infrastructure, data analysis and machine learning, embedded systems, security and reverse engineering, and prototype manufacturing. Reproducible builds with Nix are the default, so what leaves the bench can be rebuilt by someone else.
